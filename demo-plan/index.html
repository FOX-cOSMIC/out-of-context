Judge Q&A

Isn't this medical advice? What if it misreads a dose?

Agatha explains and organises; she never says keep, skip, stop or combine. Those questions go to the doctor or pharmacist, and she offers to note them. Nothing from a photo is saved until the user taps Yes; a blurry photo gets "please take it again", not a guess. In our 7 scan tests nothing was invented. Emergencies go to 112.

Where does Margaret's data go?

Plan, notes and papers stay on the iPhone, with a "Delete all my data" button. Speech recognition and the voice run on our own Mac, not a speech company. The server listens only on localhost, reached over a private Tailscale network. Claude sees the conversation, the newest photo and the plan. Production would need a hosted backend with a data agreement (EU region, no training).

Why would a 78-year-old use this when she won't use ChatGPT?

She doesn't learn an app: Agatha calls her, she just talks or holds up the paper. It starts with a job she already has every day, her pills, not "try AI". Big type, a word on every button, a patient voice that uses her name. Family is the way in: Anna installs it, Margaret keeps using it.

What's real and what's simulated?

Real: Claude with vision and tools, scanning and plan comparison, schedule and ticks, notes and papers, history and calendar answers (from demo data), local speech and voice, on a real iPhone. Simulated: the "call" is an in-app call screen with local notifications; the calendar connection is demo data; the family alert is a simulated message. Re-check this list against the build before the finals.

Business model / what next?

Families pay a monthly subscription (bought by Anna); pharmacies and care services white-label it. Germany's Medikationsplan has a barcode, so we could read it exactly. Next: real alarms, family sharing with consent, German first, a pilot with a pharmacy or senior centre.

Risks and plan B

Call flow isn't stable by recording time

Plan B: record from the Simulator with the test trigger; or drop the call beat and open on the Pills tab.

Scan answer skips "Amlodipine is new"

Plan B: record 2–3 takes, keep the one that says "new".

Run goes over 2:00

Plan B: eye letter is already out; trim the memory beat next, then shorten the hook.

Network chain fails (phone → Tailscale → Mac → Claude)

Plan B: pre-record footage in quiet conditions; Simulator talks to the Mac on loopback. Live finals: backup video at the same beat.

Remotion not approved or too slow to learn

Plan B: cut with ffmpeg (concat + drawtext overlays) or QuickTime/iMovie; keep the same timeline.

Kokoro voices sound robotic for Margaret

Plan B: a teammate records Margaret's few lines; keep Kokoro for the narrator and Agatha.

A judge thinks the family alert is real

Plan B: label it "simulated" on screen and say so in the README.

SOS on camera

Call 112 dials for real. Never tap it; only ever "Call Anna".
