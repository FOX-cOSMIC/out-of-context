Design rationale

  • Agatha is the button. One huge, friendly target in the middle of Home (and the raised centre tab everywhere else): no microphone icon to decode, just "tap me and ask".
  • She shows what she is doing. Smiling = ready, orange rings + open mouth + ear waves = listening, speaking bars + "Say it again" = answering, so a hard-of-hearing user always knows the state without relying on sound.
  • Nothing said is lost. Live words while speaking, then a "You said" bubble before the answer, so Margaret can check Agatha understood before she acts; every spoken answer is also written in large type.
  • Colour means time of day. Peach for morning, lavender for evening, mint for "done"; cards are large, rounded and labelled with sun/moon so the schedule reads at a glance, and one obvious "Yes, I took it" per due pill.
  • Warm, never childish; calm in an emergency. Flowers and the bow tie stay in the hero; text is Atkinson Hyperlegible at 18px+ in ink on light, all targets ≥56px, and SOS drops the decoration for one big red "Call 112" plus named people.
