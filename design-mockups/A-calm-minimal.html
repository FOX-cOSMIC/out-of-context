Design rationale

  • One thing per screen. Home asks a single question with a single purple button, so Margaret never has to hunt for what to do next.
  • She sees what Agatha heard. Live words while listening and a clear “You said” bubble let a slightly hard-of-hearing user catch a mistake before acting on it.
  • Answers are spoken and written. Agatha's reply is set in 27px type, with a replay control, and any action (Take) sits right below in a card with 56px+ buttons.
  • Calm, not clinical. Off-white space, soft lavender and mint, and a small bespectacled robot keep it warm; orange is saved for “now” moments, red only for SOS.
  • Stable, thumb-reachable controls. The talk pill and tab bar never move; Home sits in the centre, SOS always at the right edge, all targets at least 56px with high-contrast ink text.
